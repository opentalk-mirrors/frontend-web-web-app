Third-party fonts bundled with the OpenTalk whiteboard (Excalidraw)
===================================================================

These fonts are redistributed with the OpenTalk whiteboard from the
`@excalidraw/excalidraw` package. Most are self-hosted under
/excalidraw-assets/fonts/ (copied at build time by app/vite.config.ts);
Assistant is bundled via Excalidraw's stylesheet. Self-hosting avoids
Excalidraw's external CDN (esm.sh), which is unreachable in
network-restricted deployments.

Each font is redistributed under its own license. The full license texts are
shipped under /excalidraw-assets/licenses/ and maintained in the LICENSES/
directory of this repository.
